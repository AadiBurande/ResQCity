# Limitations and threats to validity (living document)

Each entry names the limitation, which results it can affect, and what mitigates it. Entries are
added as phases progress; the final write-up (phase 11) draws on this list.

## Data and geography (phase 2)

| # | Limitation | Affects | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Ward boundaries are hand-traced from PMC PDF maps (DataMeet); positional error of tens of metres is likely. | Ward assignment of nodes near boundaries; ward-level equity | Errors are small relative to ward sizes (median 4.6 km²); nodes are assigned by point-in-polygon with nearest-ward fallback. |
| L2 | Travel speeds: only the midday period (11–17) is calibrated to Google Maps typical times (10 routes; the baseline was faster than Google on 10/10 routes at 14:00). Peak multipliers keep their D-014 values because Google Maps did not support changing them; with 10 routes no per-hour error reduction is statistically established (D-026). Shoulder hours (06–09, 21–23) run at base speed and are probably too fast; night and class-level speeds remain assumptions. | All response-time results | Global speed scale, peak multipliers and shoulder-hour speeds are phase-10 sensitivity factors; more routes / times would tighten the calibration. |
| L3 | Spatial demand follows OSM road-class density, not observed crash locations. | Ward-level demand and **equity results**, which partly reflect how densely OSM is mapped | Alternative weighting (e.g. uniform per km of arterial road) as a phase-10 sensitivity run; stated in every equity claim. |
| L4 | Incident rate from registered figures (MoRTH 2022 Pune accidents, PMC fire calls): likely a lower bound (severe registered crashes suggest under-registration). Type mix within accidents (injury, vehicle mix) and the fire hour profile (same as road accidents, UNVERIFIED) remain assumptions. | Load on the fleet; share of multi-agency incidents (RQ2 effect size) | Sensitivity 0.5×–4× rate in phase 10; feature probabilities are UNVERIFIED sensitivity factors. |
| L5 | Flooding uses a river-distance buffer only. Nalas (storm-water streams), low spots and drainage failures are not modelled; stage fractions are illustrative. | Monsoon scenario results | Flood stages are UNVERIFIED parameters; the monsoon scenario is presented as a stress test, not a forecast. |
| L6 | Ganeshotsav routes and timings are approximate (street names, 2.5 km radius around Mandai, assumed dates). | Ganeshotsav scenario results | Route match counts are recorded; dates and routes flagged for checking against Traffic Police notices. |
| L7 | OSM facility data are incomplete or noisy: 14 fire stations, 591 `amenity=hospital` features including small nursing homes. Ambulance bases are assumed. | Fleet locations, nearest-unit times | Ambulance bases and hospital capabilities are labelled synthetic; fleet configuration (phase 6/7) selects bases explicitly. |
| L8 | Static-bucket routing: each trip uses edge times at departure (no change of speed during the trip). | Long trips spanning a period boundary | Exact time-dependent routing in phase 6.1. |
| L9 | The same rule table defines ground-truth requirements and the estimates derived from video and calls. | Could flatter CCTV-based policies | An explicit requirement-estimation error model, applied to calls and CCTV alike (phases 4 and 7). |

## Vision (phase 3)

| # | Limitation | Affects | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| L10 | Accident footage is public CCTV: UCF-Crime (mixed countries, 320×240) and ACCIDENT (216 of 1,520 test clips = 14.2 % outside the US, trimmed around the crash); normal traffic is UA-DETRAC (China). None is from Pune: domain shift in viewpoint, traffic mix (two/three-wheelers, auto-rickshaws), resolution and weather. Detection on non-US ACCIDENT clips is 0.37 vs 0.65 on US clips (D-027). | Detector recall, delay and false-alarm rates used by the simulator | Report as measured-on-public-data; non-US detection is a pre-specified pessimistic scenario and detector parameters are sensitivity factors in phase 10; re-evaluate if Pune footage becomes available. |
| L11 | UCF-Crime has only 23 onset-annotated accident videos; the v5 detector was therefore evaluated mainly on ACCIDENT (1,520 never-seen clips), whose clips are trimmed so the impact usually comes early (median 4.8 s). The first alarm can fire at 6.0 s, so most detections (542/925) fire at that instant and a content-free alarm there would score 0.69 against the detector's 0.61 (D-027). | RQ1 detection and delay; false-alarm estimates | The simulator uses detection when the camera is armed (81/138 clips with lead time) and false alarms from UA-DETRAC normal traffic; the pre-registered ACCIDENT rate is kept for the record only. |
| L12 | Default privacy mode blurs faces and an approximate plate region only (no plate detector; face detector misses small faces at 320×240). Unblurred mode exists for public-dataset demos; code can only check WHERE footage comes from (files under the public-dataset folders, or the simulated loopback cameras), not WHAT it shows, so real footage deliberately copied there or published under a simulated camera's name would not be caught. | Privacy of preview frames | Unblurred mode refused for every other source when a `CameraPipeline` is built; `full` mode available; only event metadata leaves `vision/`; coverage not measured (needs hand labels). |
| L15 | v5 detector (D-027): detection when armed 0.59 (0.50–0.67; non-US 0.35 on 31 clips); false alarms 55 per armed hour in normal traffic (UA-DETRAC, 39–77; about 29 per camera-hour with the cooldown, against 60 for a camera that alarms at every opportunity), with some cameras triggering persistently. The temporal score is no better than a constant guess and there is no evidence that pre-impact alarms anticipate crashes. The armed-camera estimate is selected on "no earlier alarm" and may differ systematically from all clips (chance level 0.15–0.27). Footage is mostly US, not Pune; the VLM verifier removes only ~23 % of validation false alarms. | RQ1 and every CCTV-triggered policy | Simulator uses `simulator_defaults` in `sim_inputs.yaml`; phase 10 varies detection 0.21–0.67, λ 39–77 per armed hour, a one-alarm-per-cooldown stress case and the share of persistently triggering cameras; fusion uses likelihood ratios. |
| L16 | No check for near-duplicate footage across splits or sources: UCF-Crime contains compilation videos, ACCIDENT and UCF-Crime both draw on public crash videos, and the training data of the TAU-R1 verifier (a traffic-camera fine-tune) were not checked for overlap with ACCIDENT clips. Within ACCIDENT, 5 clips from 3 source videos that appear on both sides of the official split were excluded. | Possible optimistic bias in val/test metrics, including the verifier's false-alarm reduction | Perceptual-hash check across sources and against the verifier's training set is future work. |
| L13 | Fire/smoke detector is trained on D-Fire (mostly wildfire/building scenes), not vehicle fires on Indian roads. | Fire event recall | Fire events carry the detector's (uncalibrated) box confidence and need persistence over several frames; treated as a separate, uncalibrated cue. |
| L14 | COCO classes have no auto-rickshaw. The UVH-26 detector (Indian classes incl. auto-rickshaw) tied with COCO on validation and was not deployed by the tie rule (D-027); its effect on Indian footage is untested (no Indian crash footage with labels). | Required-services estimate; Indian scenes | Indian test footage (e.g. IITH-Accident by e-mail request) would allow a direct check. |

## Fusion and calls (phase 4)

| # | Limitation | Affects | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| L17 | The 112 call process is UNVERIFIED: no source was found for crash-to-call delay in India, calls per incident or the share of false calls (D-028). Only the Pune call volume (23,887 Dial 112 calls in July 2026) and the 50 m handset-location scenario are cited. Because a single call crosses the 0.5 threshold on its own, the headline "median 201 s from onset to a confident incident" is close to a restatement of the assumed lognormal first-call delay (median 180 s), and fusion cannot tell a lone genuine call from a lone prank call (D-029). | Time to a confident incident on the call path; every comparison with call-based dispatch (RQ1) | All call parameters are phase-10 sensitivity factors; RQ1 is reported as a curve over the reporting delay, not one number. |
| L18 | Caller texts come from templates and the rule lexicon was written by the same authors; no real 112 transcripts. The rule triager does not generalise to unseen wording at all: its 0.33 type accuracy on held-out paraphrases is entirely the `other` fallback class being right by default, with cue recall exactly 0.0 in all three languages (against 0.94 and full cue recall on the phrasings its lexicon was written from). The offline LLM does generalise but reports cues that are absent (`results/fusion/triage_eval_synthetic.json`). | Incident type, severity and requirements estimated from calls | Triage errors are simulated (half the simulated calls use unseen phrasings); evaluation on real transcripts is future work. |
| L19 | Fusion assumptions: evidence kinds conditionally independent given the hypothesis; genuine calls are the earliest ones; at most one incident per candidate region and window (two crashes 180 m apart fuse into one — the `two_crashes_merged` replay pins this); candidates are never split or merged later; the average camera false-alarm rate is used for every camera (docs/models/fusion.md §5). The hypothesis H1 is only "at least one incident occurred somewhere in the candidate's region during its window", and a call-anchored region has a radius of up to 1,050 m, so a posterior near 1 means the incident is real, NOT that its location, type or severity are right. | Posteriors and merges; any phase-6 policy that reads the posterior as "dispatch here" | Scripted replays, a brute-force posterior check (`tests/fusion/test_posterior_calibration.py`) and tiny-instance tests verify the algebra; the synthetic-week check reports merge precision / recall, calibration and fused type / requirement accuracy. |
| L20 | Camera coverage radius (50 m), operator-review sensitivity / specificity / duration and the gate parameters are UNVERIFIED; only accidents produce simulated camera events (the fire / smoke detector is uncalibrated, L13). | Value of CCTV and of review in every policy | Phase-10 sweeps; break-even analysis of the detector quality needed (D-028). |
| L21 | The confidence model rests on 81 real and 50 false alarms in three bins fixed in advance (exploratory); the verifier score of accepted alarms carries no information (AUC 0.46). | How much "confidence-aware" can add | Reported with bootstrap intervals; at most a ~2x shift of the odds per alarm. |
| L22 | The detector masks itself: at the measured 55 false alarms per armed hour with a 60 s cooldown a camera is deaf 47.9 % of the time, so effective detection is 0.31, not 0.59 (D-029; 3 of 10 in-view incidents over 30 synthetic weeks, n = 10). | Any CCTV-triggered policy; RQ1 | The cooldown is a configured design parameter, so phase 10 sweeps it together with the false-alarm rate; a lower cooldown trades masking against alarm volume. |
| L23 | Fusion's calibration is self-consistent, not external: its prior uses the same incident rate, hotspot weights and call parameters that generated the synthetic data (`src/resqcity/sim/background.py`). A phase-10 sweep that varies those YAML values and rebuilds the prior from them keeps fusion perfectly informed at every sweep point, so **as wired it measures sensitivity to the world but not to misspecification** (D-029). | Any claim about posterior quality | Reported as an algebra check. Phase 10 must add an explicit assumed-vs-true decoupling (fusion's prior rate / hotspot / false-call parameters perturbed against the generator's, e.g. ±50 %); real calibration would need observed incidents and calls. |
| L24 | Without a camera to pin the crash time, fusion spreads the unknown onset uniformly over the window before the first call, which for an early call reaches back before the candidate's window began. Measured against a brute-force simulation: a lone late call is calibrated to within 0.01, an early one is optimistic by ~0.21 (`tests/fusion/test_posterior_calibration.py`). | Posteriors of call-only candidates, i.e. almost all of them | Pinned by a test and quantified; a camera alarm removes the approximation. Narrowing the gate window would reduce it. |
